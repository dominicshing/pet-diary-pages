Frequently asked questions

Quick answers

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No account is required. Pet Diary has no registration, sign-in or developer-operated backend. Core journal, profile, calendar, media and reminder features work without a Pet Diary server.

An internet connection or Apple service availability may be needed to turn coordinates into a readable place name. An app or service selected from the iOS share sheet may also require a connection.

Where is my information stored?

Pet Diary stores its database and app-owned media in private storage on your device. Media processing and slideshow creation also happen on the device.

Pet Diary does not provide its own cloud sync or server backup. Apple may include App data in a device backup according to your iCloud and device backup settings.

Why does Pet Diary request Photos access?

Photos access lets you browse permitted library items, select photos or videos for an entry, and save generated post cards or videos. With Limited Access, Pet Diary can see only the items permitted by iOS.

You can change access under Settings → Apps → Pet Diary → Photos, or under Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos on supported iOS versions.

Why does Pet Diary request Camera or Microphone access?

Camera access is used only when you choose to capture a photo or video. Microphone access is used for voice memories and audio recorded with video. You can change either permission under Settings → Apps → Pet Diary.

Why does Pet Diary request Location access?

Location While Using the App is used when you request a current location check-in, or enable the optional current-location setting while adding a favorite place. Pet Diary does not request background location.

Apple's system geocoding service may process coordinates to return a readable place label. Location access is optional; other App features remain available if you decline it.

Why am I not receiving reminders?
  • Confirm reminders are enabled in Pet Diary.
  • Check Settings → Notifications → Pet Diary → Allow Notifications.
  • Review reminder time, selected weekdays and event reminder settings.
  • Check whether Focus or Scheduled Summary is delaying alerts.
  • Confirm your device date, time and time zone are correct.

Reminders are scheduled locally. Notification previews may show an event title or time according to your iOS settings.

How do I share or save a memory?

Open a diary post and choose Share. Pet Diary can create a post-card image or use a completed slideshow video, then pass it to the iOS share sheet. The item is sent only to the person, app or service you select.

To save the result, use the save option in the sharing screen and allow Pet Diary to add it to Photos. The saved Photos copy is independent and is not removed if the diary entry is later deleted.

How do I delete my information?
  • Delete diary entries through their menu or edit screen.
  • Delete calendar events from the event editor.
  • Delete pets from the pet profile editor.
  • Delete favorite places from Saved Locations.
  • Remove Pet Diary to remove its local app container.

Original Photos or Files items, device backups, saved copies and previously shared content must be deleted separately. Deleting a pet does not automatically delete its earlier diary entries or events, and deleting a saved place does not remove location snapshots already attached elsewhere.

Can I transfer or restore my complete diary?

The current version does not provide full-diary export, import, account sync or a Pet Diary cloud backup. Sharing exports only the selected post card or slideshow video.

Before changing or erasing a device, review your Apple device backup settings. Pet Diary cannot guarantee that the operating system will transfer or restore App data.

I denied a permission. Can I enable it later?

Yes. Open Settings → Apps → Pet Diary and enable the Camera, Microphone, Photos, Location or Notifications permission needed for the feature. The exact path may differ slightly by iOS version.

A photo, video or audio file will not open. What can I try?
  • Confirm Pet Diary still has the required Photos or Files access.
  • Close and reopen Pet Diary.
  • Check that your device has enough free storage.
  • If an imported soundtrack is affected, select the file again.
  • Include the App version and any displayed error when contacting support.
